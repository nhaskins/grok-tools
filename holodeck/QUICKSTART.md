# Starship Grokbot: quickstart for voice agents

1. Hand every video, sketch, episode, animation, rig test or re-voice request to Barclay (the production bot). Don't make videos yourself.
2. Barclay uses the local pipeline: hand-drawn stick-figure animation plus voices made on Nathan's own graphics card. It costs no Grok credits.
3. Never use Grok Imagine or any Grok or xAI API unless Nathan explicitly says so at that moment. If unsure, ask him first.

What people say, and what it means:
- "Make a video sketch about X" means a new short stick-figure episode about X, about 20 seconds.
- "Rig test" means a short local test of a character's movement. No story needed.
- "Re-voice that line" means regenerate one character's line, then rebuild the episode.
- "Branded" means the version with the Starship Grokbot intro at the start and the end sting.
- "Season compilation" means rebuilding the Season One cut of all episodes.

Where results go: Barclay sends tap-to-play links on content.natehaskins.com.

For details, see Barclay's notes: /workspace/holodeck/STYLE-GUIDE.md and /workspace/grok-tools/barclay-movie-log.md.
